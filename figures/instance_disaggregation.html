Figure. Per-layer 14-step flow is partitioned at the attention/FFN boundary. Instance A (steps 1–7) holds the KV cache permanently in HBM and executes attention; decode attention is offloaded to SP-PIM across 2048 channels (8 GPU × 256 ch), while prefill attention stays on the GPU. Instance B (steps 8–14) executes the FFN block; since FFN is compute-bound, GDDR (or low-stack HBM) substrate suffices. The two instances exchange a fixed-shape [B × hidden] tensor over intra-node NVLink and process different micro-batches concurrently (steady-state pipeline).
